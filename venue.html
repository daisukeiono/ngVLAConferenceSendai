Access

The venue is located near JR Sendai Station, providing convenient access from Sendai Airport and major cities.

From Narita Airport (NRT)

  • Option 1 (Recommended):
    Narita Express (N'EX) to Tokyo Station (~60 min), then Tohoku Shinkansen to Sendai (~90 min).
    Total travel time: ~2.5–3 hours
  • Option 2:
    Airport Limousine Bus to Tokyo Station (~90 min), then Shinkansen to Sendai.

From Haneda Airport (HND)

  • Option 1 (Recommended):
    Tokyo Monorail to Hamamatsucho, then JR Yamanote Line to Tokyo Station, followed by Tohoku Shinkansen to Sendai.
    Total travel time: ~2–2.5 hours
  • Option 2:
    Domestic flight from Haneda to Sendai Airport (~1 hour), then Sendai Airport Access Line to Sendai Station (~25 min).

From Sendai Station

The Sendai SME Activation Center is directly connected to JR Sendai Station (West Exit).
