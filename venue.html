Hotels

There are many reasonable hotels located near Sendai station. Please use your favorite booking site to reserve your room.

Access

The venue is conveniently located near JR Sendai Station. For participants traveling from Asia, direct flights to Sendai Airport may be available and offer the most convenient option. For those arriving from other regions, we recommend flying into Haneda or Narita Airport in Tokyo, traveling to Tokyo Station, and then taking the Tohoku Shinkansen to Sendai.
