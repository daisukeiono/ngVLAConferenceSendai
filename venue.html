Access

The venue is located near JR Sendai Station, providing convenient access from Sendai Airport and major cities.

Recommended:
Access via Tokyo Station and the Tohoku Shinkansen is the most convenient and reliable option.

Alternative:
Flights from Haneda Airport to Sendai Airport are available, followed by a 25-minute train ride to Sendai Station.
